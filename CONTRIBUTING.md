# Contributing to Shipping Rate Calculator

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Shipping Rate Calculator project! Whether it's reporting a bug, suggesting an enhancement, or improving the documentation, your help is greatly appreciated.

## Code of Conduct

By participating in this project, you agree to abide by our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Please treat all contributors with respect.

## How to Contribute

### Reporting Bugs

If you find a bug, please help us by opening a new issue on GitHub. When submitting an issue, please be sure to include:
- A clear, descriptive title.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- The expected behavior and the actual behavior.
- Details about your environment (browser, OS, etc.).

### Suggesting Enhancements

Have an idea to make the shipping calculator even better? We'd love to hear it! Please open an issue to propose your ideas. Be sure to describe your suggestion and why you think it would be a valuable addition.

### Pull Requests

If you are ready to submit a change or fix:
1. Fork the repository and create your feature branch.
2. Ensure your changes follow the project's coding standards.
3. Submit a pull request detailing the changes you've made.
